Overtime HOD default update

Upload these two files to the matching paths in your PMS installation:
application/models/Overtime_model.php
application/views/overtime/create.php

No SQL migration or existing controller changes are required for this update.

Team leaders of active company teams and users with an active administrator
role default to their active department's HOD in the same business location.
Explicit Overtime Reporting Leaders overrides take priority. If no department
HOD is configured, the existing company-team mapping is used.
Other employees continue to use their company-team leader.

A configured HOD who is inactive, missing, in another location or the requester
cannot be used. Correct the HOD or assign a reporting override under Task Master.
The selected approver still needs Overtime Approvals + Edit permission.
The two approval stages and independent final administrator remain required.
Existing requests retain their saved approval route.

Local PHP HOD, workflow, permissions and rendering checks passed on SQLite.
Live deployment and verification have not been performed.
