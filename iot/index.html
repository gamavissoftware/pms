A rising trend across identical cycles is the earliest warning of a dulling tool — visible here before it shows up as a reject.
What we do not offer, and why Automatic tool-wear offset correction is not included. It writes values back into a machine that is cutting, which carries real safety and liability exposure and sits outside MTConnect — a read-only standard. The system computes and recommends the offset; an operator applies it. We would advise against any supplier offering automatic write-back.
